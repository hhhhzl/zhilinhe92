# Research gallery sources

Metadata and conference/submission status follow the author-provided CV. The IET book chapter is excluded.

Author-provided research material, reused at the author’s request. Retain upstream paper and repository terms; this record does not grant a new license. Prior publication figure licensing is recorded separately in sources.json.

Some author-provided 6-second showcase derivatives were already cropped, resized and time-normalized before this task; their original manifests are retained as context. These clips are qualitative demonstrations, not real-time performance evidence.

Representative round-100 policy rollouts use an environment/method-selected seed; the displayed episode is not an aggregate success metric or evidence of cross-embodiment policy transfer.

All 37 video files are H.264. JPEG posters are decoded from the corresponding video; no research media were synthesized.

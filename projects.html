01 / RESEARCH FRAMEWORKDEC 2025 — PRESENT

Generative Dynamics

A shared foundation for robots that
plan, learn, and respond.

An open-source framework connecting generative optimization, robot learning, and feedback control. Modular task, solver, and execution interfaces make algorithms interchangeable, with reproducible workflows spanning navigation, arm avoidance, locomotion, and contact-rich manipulation.

Tennis Buddy

More time playing.
Less time picking up.

A ROS2-based autonomous robot for tennis-ball retrieval. It brings perception, mapping, and navigation together to detect balls, move between waypoints, and avoid obstacles.

How does experience
become physical intelligence?

I’m Zhilin (Hector) He, a master’s student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. I study robot learning, generative optimization, and feedback control—how robots can turn learned experience into actions that work in the physical world.

My work brings dynamics, geometry, and contact feedback into the process of generating behavior, spanning multi-robot planning, legged locomotion, and contact-rich manipulation. I also study how heterogeneous learners can share experience while preserving their own capabilities.

I build Generative Dynamics to connect these ideas in a reusable robotics framework. Previously, I studied Applied and Computational Mathematics at USC and worked as a software development engineer at Reli.

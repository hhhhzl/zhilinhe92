Digital performer material notes, 2026-10-16

Human Skin cinematic PBR: projected authorized likeness albedo, UV roughness
map with softer T-zone/lip response, and a baked tangent normal map combining
the original detailed surface with fine procedural relief. Retain these maps
in the web renderer; do not force a constant roughness over the face. The small
Cycles subsurface component is for Blender preview only and is not claimed as
a glTF skin-scattering implementation.

Human Eyes iris and sclera PBR: the original rigged eyeball faces (756 faces)
have their own opaque textured surface, roughness 0.24 and IOR 1.376. There is
no added transparent cornea cover. This avoids the large white reflections
observed with transmission over the source eye geometry in WebGL.

Human Hair photographic cards: the built-in ImageGen RGBA atlas is preserved,
alpha MASK cutoff 0.20, double-sided, roughness 0.73, anisotropy 0.65, rotation
pi/2, with a native Blender baked strand normal map. Human Hair underlayer
samples only the atlas's dense opaque fiber region. Human Hair fine flyaways
adds sparse irregular silhouette strands. Do not replace MASK with BLEND or
turn off depth writes for the hair cards. Prompts: hair-atlas-prompt.txt.

Human Polo cotton pique PBR: blue/ivory bands baked to the original clothing
UVs, subtle geometric torso folds, normal-mapped cotton relief and sheen.
Human Polo soft cotton collar: thin 0.6 mm folded collar geometry, reduced
arch and short soft points, positioned above the clothing to avoid clipping.

The human's identity geometry, original rig, 5-second Echo, neutral wrists,
0.12-second robot response delay, planted feet and eye anchors are retained.
This is an improved real-time digital performer, not a scanned film double.

Stylized adult character — selected art direction translated to native 3D

Reference: concepts/zhilin-cartoon-sheet.png. Built-in ImageGen produced the
selected character sheet from the owner's supplied photographs and a written
cartoon art brief. Exact generation prompts are in concepts/PROMPTS.txt.
This is an artistic character interpretation, not a scan or a claim of exact
photographic likeness. The reference sheet is not used as a billboard or as
projected face texture in the scene.

Head: head_refinement.py constructs the wider rounded cheeks and chin, short
rounded nose, integrated lip volumes, soft smile, ears and sculpted brows.
Actual eye openings are bridged into the head topology with continuous skin
surfaces; all new lid vertices follow the original Head bone. Brown irises and
pupils are mapped to true spherical opaque eyes. The lid openings overlap the
iris rather than leaving a white ring around it. Thin champagne glasses remain
full geometry. The eye anchors and the existing choreography are unchanged.

Hair: hair_refinement.py constructs a continuous scalp foundation and twelve
asymmetric swept S/C-wave locks. Clump roots and undersides embed into the
foundation; fine directional relief defines the flow without alpha cards or
floating rings. These are deliberately sculpted cartoon waves, not a claim to
match individual strands in the concept illustration. Native vertex colors
supply restrained warm/cool variation through glTF COLOR_0 with a white factor.

Body and clothing: the licensed Microsoft Rocketbox body and rig supply hands,
underlying body, weights and stable authored movement. Original head/hair are
removed. garment_refinement.py adds bounded cloth smoothing and broad folds
while preserving skin/hand anchors and interpolating weights on subdivision.
The new continuous rolled collar, rounded lapels and fitted narrow placket
replace the old trim. A thin navy facing bridges the neck/collar clearance,
blending Neck/Spine2 weights. Polo and trousers receive FabricUV coordinates.

Runtime maps (all packed into .blend and embedded into the GLB):
- concept-skin-tone.png: native Blender color bake, 2048 px; softly authored
  cheek/nose/lip/orbit coloration, no photographic projection or pore texture.
- concept-eye-left.png and concept-eye-right.png: native Blender color bakes,
  1024 px; sclera, layered brown iris and pupil on each spherical eye.
- stylized-polo-stripes.png: native Blender broad-stripe color bake, 4096 px.
- cloth-cotton-normal.png / cloth-cotton-roughness.png: 512 px garment maps.
- cloth-twill-normal.png / cloth-twill-roughness.png: 512 px trouser maps.
Normal/roughness textures bind directly to glTF PBR slots on FabricUV. Color
maps are baked in Blender; cloth maps use deterministic numerical weave patterns
saved through Blender image data. No photo editing or external texture is used.
Bake targets isolate a single material slot to avoid clearing unrelated maps.
The exporter may combine roughness into a metallic-roughness image (G channel).

Key materials: Human Concept softly colored skin; Human Concept brown eye -1;
Human Concept brown eye 1; Human Concept layered espresso waves;
Human Stylized champagne frames; Human Stylized blue ivory polo;
Human Stylized soft ivory collar; Human Stylized charcoal trousers.
Skin roughness 0.66, eye roughness 0.23, hair roughness 0.57 are intentional
constants. All character surfaces use opaque standard PBR; no transmission
cornea, alpha hair, runtime toon filter or flat material override is needed.
Only architectural glass uses transparency.

Animation: one five-second Echo source clip, unchanged from the neutral-wrist
revision. Wrists stay neutral, fingers relaxed, feet planted; quaternion signs
are continuous and the loop closes. Robot source delay is 0.12 seconds.
Runtime playback speed is set by the website separately.

Lighting: retain the site's indigo/peach hall lighting and subject-tracked focus.
Neutral comparison and original scene renders use the same near-face camera.

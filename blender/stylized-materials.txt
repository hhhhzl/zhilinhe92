Stylized adult character — selected art direction translated to native 3D

Reference: concepts/zhilin-cartoon-sheet.png. Built-in ImageGen produced the
selected character sheet from the owner's supplied photographs and a written
cartoon art brief. Exact generation prompts are in concepts/PROMPTS.txt.
This is an artistic character interpretation, not a scan or a claim of exact
photographic likeness. The reference sheet is not used as a billboard or as
projected face texture in the scene.

Geometry: the adult face, small rounded nose, modeled smile, ears, almond eyes,
sculpted brows, champagne glasses and solid asymmetrical wave hair are new native
meshes. The eye white, iris and pupil share a continuous opaque surface. Hair is
closed solid geometry with fused broad waves, not alpha-masked cards. The
licensed Microsoft Rocketbox body and rig supply the hands, underlying body,
skin weights and stable authored movement. The original face and hair
have been removed. The shirt opening is bridged to its actual mesh boundary;
its new vertices interpolate the body skin weights. Continuous cuffs cover the
source garment boundary.

Materials: Human Stylized warm peach skin; Human Stylized ivory sclera;
Human Stylized chestnut iris; Human Stylized pupil; Human Stylized solid waves 0;
Human Stylized champagne frames; Human Stylized blue ivory polo;
Human Stylized soft ivory collar; Human Stylized continuous blue neckline;
Human Stylized charcoal trousers; Human Stylized ivory sneakers.
All character surfaces use standard opaque glTF PBR materials. No transmission
cornea, alpha hair, facial photo projection, skin pore map or fabric micro-normal
is used. Only the native procedural broad-stripe polo color is UV baked to a
4096-pixel PNG, packed into the .blend and embedded in the GLB. Baking isolates
one material slot so no unrelated image can be cleared.

Animation: one five-second Echo source clip, unchanged from the neutral-wrist
revision. Wrist rotations remain neutral, fingers relaxed, feet planted;
quaternion signs are continuous and the loop closes. The robot's source delay
is 0.12 seconds. Runtime playback speed is set by the website separately.

Lighting: retain the site's indigo/peach hall lighting and subject-tracked focus.
No extra runtime toon filter or flat material override is required. The soft
surface response and simplified colors are authored into the 3D material set.

Sources: Microsoft Rocketbox Male_Adult_02 (MIT), body and rig; MuJoCo Menagerie
Unitree G1 (BSD-3-Clause), robot geometry/kinematics. See the public asset license
files. Original choreography and cartoon appearance are authored for this site.
